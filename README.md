One joke about Monday mornings, written by `main.py` to `entry.txt`.

> My bed filed a formal complaint about Monday mornings. HR agreed the bed had a point — then scheduled a 9 a.m. meeting about it.

The entry is a single joke (128 characters), well inside the 200-character limit.
The joke turns a Monday-morning complaint into office bureaucracy: the bed
files a grievance, HR sides with the bed, and the punchline is that the
supposed remedy is the very thing everyone dreads — a 9 a.m. meeting.
